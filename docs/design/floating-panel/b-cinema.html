B · Cinema

Reads like film subtitles. The current line is large and centred, and older lines fade upward into the meeting. There's no chrome until you hover.

What changes

  • No header bar. A status chip appears on hover, and stays up for a notice or click-through.
  • The live English is set at 21pt semibold, centred. History stays at 15pt above it.
  • The background is a veil, transparent at the top and dark at the bottom. Hovering makes the panel solid for reading back.
  • Japanese is set in Hiragino Mincho, so it reads as a gloss rather than a second subtitle.
  • "translating…" becomes three pulsing dots, and the hearing line gets a small level meter.

SwiftUI

A LinearGradient background with .mask on the history ScrollView, .onHover for the chrome, and .multilineTextAlignment(.center). Medium effort.

Trade-off

Centred text is harder to scan back through than left-aligned. Without a hover, the status isn't visible at a glance.
