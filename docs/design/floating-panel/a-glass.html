A · Glass HUD

The closest to macOS itself: it looks like Control Centre or a Now Playing HUD. Same structure as today, but the meeting shows through the panel.

What changes

  • Flat 82% black becomes a dark vibrancy material, so the panel lets the meeting show through.
  • Older history rows fade (50% → 75% → 100%). Hovering or reading back brings them to full.
  • The live pane is an inset card with a blue bar, not a tinted band.
  • The header gains a backend chip. The history pane fades out at the top edge.

SwiftUI

.background(.ultraThinMaterial) with .environment(\.colorScheme, .dark). The recency fade is an opacity by index. Low effort.

Trade-off

Contrast depends on what's behind the panel: a white slide makes the glass lighter. Raise the material tint if that's a problem.
