Glossary correction on every row

What changed in Tsuyaku 0.7.0 → 0.7.2 on the Gemini Live backend, and why. Interactive: press the buttons to play each animation.

The problem in one line: Gemini Live can't be given your glossary, so ラクスル comes out as "Lacsul", "Rax" or not at all. Apple Intelligence on your Mac fixes the English after the row appears. Version 0.6.0 only did that when the Japanese contained the term exactly, and Gemini's Japanese often mishears the term itself, so most mistakes were never fixed.

0.7.0

Check every row

  • Every Japanese row goes to Apple Intelligence, not only exact matches
  • The rewriter is told misheard terms count (ラクスール, "Lux")
  • A rewrite must add a glossary term the line lacked
  • Chevron reveals the original English
  • Notice when the glossary is too large for the model
0.7.1

Tint only the fixed words

  • Words Apple Intelligence wrote: light blue
  • Gemini's own words stay white
  • Word-by-word diff (CorrectionDiff)
0.7.2

No time limit

  • The 3 s cutoff is gone
  • Every row waits for its correction, however long
  • Fixes "I don't see anything rewritten"

Only when the row's Japanese, or the previous row's, contains a glossary term exactly.

Now (0.7.0+)

Every Japanese row with English is checked, when you have a glossary and Apple Intelligence.

Gemini's JapaneseChecked?

English echo rows are still never rewritten: they are what was actually said.

3. When a rewrite is kept

Checking every row means Apple Intelligence sees many lines with nothing to fix. To stop it "improving" Gemini's phrasing, a rewrite is kept only if all of these hold:

  1. It is non-empty and differs from Gemini's English.
  2. It contains the English of a glossary term that Gemini's line did not have.
  3. Its length is within about 30% of the original (at least 6 characters of slack).

Rules

  • Light blue: words Apple Intelligence wrote. White: Gemini's words.
  • The chevron is per row and collapsed by default. It isn't remembered after Clear or relaunch.
  • It needs Click-Through off, like any click on the panel.
  • Saved and copied transcripts get the corrected English only: no original, no marker.
  • The Translate My Voice caption panel is unchanged.

How the blue words are found (0.7.1)

The two lines are lined up word by word (longest common subsequence). Words in both stay white; words only in the correction turn blue. A space between two blue words is blue too, so the fixed phrase reads as one.

5. Why nothing was rewritten — and the fix in 0.7.2

Corrections did work, but they arrived too late. Each row sends Apple Intelligence the whole glossary (322 terms, about 3,000 tokens), and reading that takes the on-device model about 3–4 s. The old corrector kept Gemini's English if a correction wasn't back within 3 s, so it threw almost every one away.

The trade-off of no limit

  • Every correction lands, and the transcript always gets the corrected English.
  • Rows reach the transcript later: about 3–4 s per row with your glossary.
  • Corrections run one at a time. In fast speech they fall behind, and held rows can pile up in the live pane.
  • Stop still settles every held row at once, with whatever English it has.

6. The "glossary too large" notice

Apple's on-device model has a 4,096-token context. At Start, Tsuyaku measures the glossary's instructions and keeps 600 tokens free for the row and the reply. If they don't fit, glossary correction is turned off and the header says so. This only works on macOS 26.4 and later, where the model can count tokens.

7. Possible next step

Instead of sending all 322 terms with every row, send only the handful that sound like something in the row. Comparing in romaji catches kanji and katakana mishearings alike:

HeardRomajiGlossary term
楽するとrakusurutoラクスル → rakusuru
ラックスがrakkusugaラクスル → rakusuru (close)
ラクスールrakusūruラクスル → rakusuru (close)

That would bring each correction down to about 0.4 s, so rows would no longer pile up, and the size limit would stop mattering. It is not built yet.
